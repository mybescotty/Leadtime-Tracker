Track every bus off the road, the parts it is waiting on, and when it went back into service.
Tab 1 – Parts (one row per part): Date Requested · Date Ordered · Bus Number · Part Number · Part Description · Comments · Date Due · Date Arrived
Tab 2 – Buses (one row per day): Date · Bus Numbers on Log (comma separated, e.g. 12345, 12343MOT, 12121 – add MOT after the number for buses in for MOT)
A bus is off road from the first day it appears on the daily log until the first logged day it is missing (back on road).
Parts are matched to a VOR spell by bus number and request date.
